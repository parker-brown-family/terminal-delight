- 💡 Queries a Kitty-graphics host must answer: (1) a=q query: reply `ESC _Gi=<id>;OK ESC \`, or `ENOENT:<msg>` or another error code. It must reply immediately and must not store the image. - 💡 Unicode placeholders are the multiplexer-safe form of the Kitty protocol.
