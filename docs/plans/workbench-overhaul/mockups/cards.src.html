What the reader can act on

Use the same place for the title and actions on every card. A narrow table can repeat its column names down the page. A changeset can put each patch beneath its file. The reader can scroll through the evidence while the rail keeps the other cards within reach.

Which reply deserves a closer look

The Overview belongs to one pane. Give each reply a headline and a short preview. Keep the full plain-English answer and its evidence on the card. A keyboard reader can move between replies, inspect evidence and choose a specific action.
