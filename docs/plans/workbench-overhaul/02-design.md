# Reading and operating the workbench

The card opens on the work's name and the actions its author supports. A decision puts the question above Approve and Reject. The recommendation, case, cost and consequences follow in the same order in a tiled pane and a wide one. At 1,600 px two options share a row. At 600 px they stack. Architecture groups wrap by the same principle. Estimates sit below the evidence. A blocked response keeps its existing escalation at the top.

The Overview belongs to one conversation. It gives the current and selected reply room for a preview. Older replies use their headlines. The shelf and the document scroll separately, so reading an old reply keeps its headline in view. Wide prose uses a maximum line width of 850 layout pixels. A table can use the full width. At 600 px each table record repeats its column labels alongside its values.

Architecture cards keep their edges, changesets their patches, artifacts their additional fields, and Markdown its ending. Unknown kinds show the original payload. A missing table cell uses the localized word for unavailable, while an explicitly empty string draws an empty value.

## Keyboard

Up and Down move through the shelf. Left and Right move a visible outline through the controls on screen. Enter activates the outlined control, including the individual hunk named on a review button. PgUp and PgDn page through the card. Tab and Alt+1 through Alt+4 keep their existing shelf shortcuts. Typing in the composer keeps the text editor's cursor keys. The help sheet lists the new reading keys in all nine languages.
