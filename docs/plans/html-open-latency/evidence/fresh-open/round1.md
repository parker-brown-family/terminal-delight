I found two lifecycle regressions in `HEAD~1..HEAD` by tracing the code. I did not reproduce either at runtime.

1. **Medium — CONFIRMED: closing or hiding a view during the fused first tile can leave its Chromium page open until idle shutdown.** `app/src/docview/page.rs:811` starts tile capture after `open_if_wanted` has made its last cancellation check. If the view closes or a newer render replaces it during capture or decode, the foreground task is dropped; the background result has no later cancellation check or page close. The page was inserted into the engine, but `adopt` never records it in `p.live`, so the view’s release path cannot close it.

2. **Low — CONFIRMED: idle shutdown can retain a stale “ready page.”** In `app/src/docview/snapshot.rs:760`, the ready-page worker lowers `in_flight` before publishing its `Blank`. Idle shutdown can then clear `ready` and take the browser (`snapshot.rs:1097`), after which the worker inserts the blank while the CDP connection has not yet closed. Shutdown does not clear `ready` again. `page_ready()` can report true with no browser, and the retained `Blank` holds its CDP handle until another open or engine drop.

I found no other concrete regression in picture/layout pairing, document-context isolation, network blocking, `Pending` reply cleanup, `FileChanged` retry, stale generations, scroll targets, or decode handling. The two screenshot timing questions in the work order remain unverified. No runtime tests were run; this was a read-only source review.
