# Product: Work that remains reviewable

A delivered document remains findable when its pane closes. Rewriting a document must not make a note appear to endorse a different passage. Closing a terminal explicitly must finish cleanup, even when its program ignores hangup.

The existing live artifact rail stays a current-attention surface. Its archive action opens a persistent index of handed-over files, including closed conversations. Each entry reports available, missing or unknown state and offers retained revisions where observed. Existing handovers have unknown past content until actually measured.

Markdown notes retain the text and document revision the person reviewed. Moved text can resolve again; missing quotations remain outdated; indistinguishable candidates remain ambiguous. Legacy notes are retained with their original identity, without fabricating quotation evidence.

Success is verified with disposable processes, files, notes and restart/reload fixtures. No user's live process or note is a test fixture.
