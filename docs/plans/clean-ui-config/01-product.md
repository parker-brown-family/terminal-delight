# Product: Clean, and UI CONFIG

## Problem

Parker, on the first Clean workshop (2026-10-21): *"We missed a major issue with CLEAN.. how far it should reach... Literally almost everywhere... All those menu settings and even the little glyphs for menus at the bottom right need to be TOGGLABLE for visibility."*

A fresh Terminal Delight window shows every control it has, all the time, and lights most of them. Someone who wants a plain, quiet working surface has no way to put the chrome away. The only "plain" option today, Quiet, changes the screen's finish and none of the chrome. Clean as a screen finish alone was never going to read as clean: on a fresh window, four of the six designs already look almost alike.

## Success metric

**The number of controls a new window shows at rest**, counted on a fresh install with one tab and two panes: today against Clean. Measured by photographing a fresh window in each state and counting distinct controls, a control in a pane header counting once per pane. First count, from the inventory of the running build: **36 today, 8 in the proposed Clean set** (one tab, two panes, left bar open, allowance records present; method in the Gate 1 page's "How 36 and 8 were counted"). The target is fewer than half of today's, with nothing reachable today becoming unreachable.

## Announcement — the post before the feature

Terminal Delight now opens Clean: flat, unlit and nearly square, with your panes, their names and their state, and very little else. Everything else moves into one CONFIG menu in the corner. The painter palette's new UI CONFIG section shows any of it again, for the window or for every pane at once. Prefer the glow? Hacker and Intrusive are a click away in DESIGN, and you can make any look the one your new windows start with.
