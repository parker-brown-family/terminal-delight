TD's settings live under ⚙ CONFIG, so the panel opens from there as UPDATES, drawn here in the shape of the existing ADMIN overlay. Version numbers in the stable mockups are illustrative. The dev commit is the real head of dev/0.5.0 today.

Where it stands

The whole design rests on one fact found in the code: your terminals live in the session host, a separate process that the update does not replace. So almost every update swaps only the window, and the terminals never notice. The rare update that changes how the window talks to the host is the only one that can cost terminals, and it waits for you (figure 1). You answered the one question (it ships in 0.5.0), and your note about the cutover added section 09. Everything marked amber is a call I made, and any of it can be overturned.

09What survives the cutover, and what is backed up

You asked whether we are 100% sure nobody loses settings, agent state or running processes. Not 100% today. Figure 2 splits everything TD keeps into what has been measured to survive, what will be backed up, what is known to be lost and must be named, and what nobody has listed yet.
