# Can I read the menu and make the choice?

At 600 pixels I can open the Floor, see who acts and pick a verb. I cannot read whom the line reaches. In Appearance I can see a picture of a terminal, but the name of the setting beside it runs down the page a letter at a time. Both work at 1600 pixels. These are the first two repairs.

## What to fix first

| Rank | Where I get stuck | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Edit a line.** The Floor gives the actor and verb fixed widths. At 600 the target collapses against the pane pool, even on a saved line. TERMINIMALIST’s words run across that boundary. | Give targets a second row at tiled widths. Keep the actor, verb and target names together. |
| 2 | **Find a setting.** Under Appearance, the preview and Open button keep their widths while the title and explanation lose theirs. Several other pages use the same row. | Wrap the sample and control before the words lose their reading width. |
| 3 | **Copy, paste or clear.** The pane shows Copy without a selection. All three commands hide the keys that Help already lists. Link gestures in the same menu do show theirs. | Put the shortcuts at the right of the actions. Offer Copy when there is something selected. |
| 4 | **Check pane access.** The pane and Floor use the same three labels, but even in the text skin the only difference between the chosen row and the others is a small filled circle. | Use the existing translated word for “selected” in TERMINIMALIST. Keep the setting and its name the same in both places. |

Priority follows the cost of guessing. A missing target can change which agent receives a relationship. An unreadable heading prevents finding a setting. The other two slow down familiar actions. We have no measurements of how often people open these menus.

## Walking the window

| Place | What the two widths and skins show |
| --- | --- |
| Window corner | Default puts Settings, size, design and gauges side by side. TERMINIMALIST leads with Settings and names the controls inside. Config, design and gauges have distinct jobs. The long design tray needs scrolling at either width. |
| Pane context | Paste and Clear scrollback follow Copy. Link actions depend on what was clicked. Access follows a dividing rule. The badge beside the current access setting deserves the same treatment on a Floor card. |
| Tab context | Name, group and project describe where the tab lives. The hotkey hint says how to mark a pane. Its instructions take up most of the row in a tile. A pin and an assigned number are two different states. |
| Left bar | The header menu begins with New project and New group, then offers to adopt loose tabs. “Nothing to recover” is information styled as a menu item. Project, group and task share Promote and Demote at the top, with rename and removal further down. Branch deletion names the number of groups, tabs and panes affected. The closed bar’s project chip switches branches. |
| Floor | A card menu names the pane. Removing it from the org appears before inspecting its access choices. Choosing a drawn line opens its relationship for editing. The verb list spells out what the actor can do. The narrow editor hides the target even though the line and its two cards remain visible below. |
| Composer | The pane shows the model and effort above the reply field. Names stay legible in the wide pane. At 600, the open left bar takes a large share of the window and the strip has very little space for its later controls. An armed message still shows where it goes. |
| Help | Categories group commands with their shortcuts. This is a good source for the pane menu’s key spelling. Filtering is easier than scanning the whole sheet. |
| Settings | General and Interface offer choices, Window chrome and On every pane offer switches. Appearance pairs explanatory text, previews and Open. Keybindings names F1 and the Settings shortcut. Providers, Threads, Projects and Worktrees use action and timing rows. Sharing & access distinguishes a switch from a limit. Environments and Archive show an empty state when there is nothing to choose. Maintenance, Source control, Notifications, Labs and About have short lists. The right column, especially rows with samples, is what loses room first. |
