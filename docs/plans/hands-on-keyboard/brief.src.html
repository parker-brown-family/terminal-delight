Hands on Keyboard

A tester on X missed tmux-style keyboard scrolling in Terminal Delight, and you asked for a key that opens the current artifact and a better way to move around thirty tabs than the mouse. Half of it already exists; four choices are yours, and three ideas go further.

01 The Alt row is nearly full

Terminal Delight owns ten Alt+letters. Your shell and the agent prompts own most of the rest, because readline has used them for forty years.

02 Pane marks work like car radio presets

Your idea: Alt+3 goes to the pane marked 3, and holding it marks the pane you are on. Car radios work this way, and they teach one lesson: a tap acts when the key comes up.

A mark follows the agent's conversation, so it survives a sleep and wake. An empty mark says "3 is empty, hold to set"; a closed one says "3 was KEYS · hands on keyboard, closed". Alt+0 goes back to where you came from.

03 Scroll mode moves by meaning

Alt+[, tmux's key with Alt for the prefix, puts the pane in scroll mode: letters move you instead of going to the program. Terminal Delight knows which lines are yours, the agent's, tool calls and links, so you can move by block.

04 Three wild ones

Marks need setting up. These three do not.

05 Yours to settle

Which key opens the current artifact?

The pane's newest handed-over page opens in the floating square, and the key closes it again. With nothing handed over it says so rather than guessing.

Recommend Alt+O. Alt+B works as you asked, at the price of back-one-word in every shell and agent prompt.

Do Alt+1–9 become pane marks?

Today they jump to tab positions, and are on unless switched off.

Recommend yes: positions mean little past nine tabs. Alt+Shift+letter tab pins stay; the workbench shelf gets a test first.

Does holding a mark key set it, or peek at it?

Peeking (that pane floats over yours until you let go) would be used more often, but needs the same gesture.

Recommend hold to set, as you described. Peeking happens inside the Constellation instead: the first letter you type previews that pane big.

What ships first?

Four slices, each one usable alone.

Recommend: 1 · Shift+PgUp in shell panes and the open-artifact key. 2 · pane marks and Alt+0. 3 · scroll mode. 4 · the Constellation. Afterglow and lookup-by-work after that. A tmux preset with a Ctrl+B prefix waits until someone asks, since Ctrl+B is also readline's back-one-character.
