Terminal Delight is a terminal made for Linux, and for Omarchy in particular: a native app, written in Rust on gpui and alacritty_terminal, drawn by your GPU. It is a real terminal first, with tiling panes, tabs, session restore, and a CRT look you can dial up or down. When a pane is running a coding agent, it reads that agent's own transcript and shows you what the agent is doing, what it has spent, and whether it is waiting on you.

On Omarchy

Every Omarchy theme installed on your machine is already in Terminal Delight. Press Super+Alt+P and a paint overlay rises over the window with all of them on it. One letter paints the outer shell, or just the terminal you're in, so each pane can wear a different theme from its neighbours.

Omarchy · Super + Alt + PPaint the shell and every terminal in your own themes →

The chord comes with Terminal Paint, the 🎨 in the Omarchy bar. Without it, terminal-delight ctl paint on raises the same overlay. Themes has every key.
