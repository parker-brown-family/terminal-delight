Terminal Delight keeps its settings in ~/.config/terminal-delight/, its records in ~/.local/state/terminal-delight/, and its sockets in your runtime directory. $XDG_CONFIG_HOME, $XDG_STATE_HOME and $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR move them.

The files you're likely to edit by hand are theme.toml and skin.toml, and both are picked up the moment you save. Everything else is written by the app when you change something in it: the session layouts, launch defaults, notification settings, favourites, and which logo goes with which directory.

Technical lists every file, what writes it and what reads it.

TD_THEME and TD_SKIN point the theme and skin at other files.

~/.local/state/terminal-delight/

PathHolds
surfaces/<session>/<pane>/The workbench's surfaces, up to 512 per pane, and the agent channel's files: actions.jsonl, inbound.jsonl, outbound.jsonl, answers/, bench.json.
conversations/The bench's record, kept by conversation, so it outlives windows.
agents/usage/<id>.jsonOne usage record per subscription. See Usage and vitals.
agent-ledger/Which conversation each agent process is in, written by the recovery hook. This one is always under ~/.local/state, whatever $XDG_STATE_HOME says.
brief-backups/Copies taken when notes on a document are saved.

Elsewhere

PathHolds
$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/terminal-delight/ctl-<pid>.sock per window; session-<name>.sock, .log, .tag and .window per session host.
$XDG_CACHE_HOME/terminal-delight/pages/Rendered HTML documents.
~/.local/share/context-delight/Harvested sessions, as .cdx.

Files that hold your working directories and resume commands are owner-only (0600) in owner-only directories (0700). To clear what Terminal Delight remembers, delete sessions/. The finish bell has no file: its sound is built in.

  1. The config root: instance.rs; each file's reader is named in the source beside it: theme.rs, skin.rs, launchpref.rs, notifpref.rs, dirlogo.rs, fav.rs, surfacefeed.rs, benchstore.rs, usage.rs.
