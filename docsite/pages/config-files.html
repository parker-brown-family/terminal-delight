Terminal Delight keeps its settings in ~/.config/terminal-delight/, its records in ~/.local/state/terminal-delight/, rendered pages in ~/.cache/terminal-delight/, the decision-brief kit it hands agents in ~/.local/share/terminal-delight/, and its sockets in your runtime directory. The XDG_ variables move each of them.

The files you might edit by hand are theme.toml and skin.toml, which are picked up the moment you save, plus documents.toml, tool-props.toml and local-agent.toml. Everything else is written by the app when you change something in it: session layouts, launch defaults, notification switches, favourites, and which logo goes with which directory.

Your sessions are in sessions/, with the last ten versions of each kept beside them. Deleting that folder clears what Terminal Delight remembers about your windows.

Technical lists every file, what creates it and when, and what reloads while the app runs.

Files that hold your working directories and resume commands are written whole and then swapped in, owner-only (0600) in owner-only folders (0700), so a crash mid-save never leaves half a file.1

The runtime directory is emptied when you log out.

Outside its own folders

  • Claude Code's settings.json gets the briefing hook, only after you say yes, with a copy of the old file kept beside it as settings.json.td-backup.<seconds>.
  • ~/.config/xdg-terminals.list is written by terminal-delight default-terminal on and off, and by nothing else.
  • A decision brief's own HTML file is rewritten in place when you save notes into it, after a copy goes to brief-backups/.
  • Sharing a demo of a layout writes a snapshot to $TMPDIR/td-demo-<pid>-<time>.toml, owner-only. Nothing deletes it.
  • A picture a program sends through a temporary file is read and then deleted, as the Kitty graphics protocol asks.
  • Resting an agent freezes its pane's cgroup and asks the kernel to push its memory out, through that cgroup's own files under /sys/fs/cgroup.

It reads, and never writes: agent transcripts under ~/.claude/projects/ and ~/.codex/sessions/; Omarchy's themes, its hidden-themes list and its usage records; and on 0.5.0, Claude Code's ~/.claude/sessions/.2

Where XDG variables are ignored

agent-ledger/ is always under ~/.local/state, and context-delight/ and demo-logos/ are always under ~/.local/share. Most state paths use $XDG_STATE_HOME only when it is an absolute path; a few accept a relative one. With $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR unset, the control sockets and the session sockets fall back to different folders under /tmp.
