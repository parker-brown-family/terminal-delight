Terminal Light

You asked for more glow and dimmer text in inactive panes, plus the fading letters missing from the first build. Your test window on workspace 4 now has both, with organised GAUGES. The new tab uses OPS and your captured picture settings.

Afterglow

New text appears immediately. Replaced letters fade at their previous positions for 180 milliseconds by default. The slider runs from 0 to 1,200 ms; zero clears the trail. Letter glow and afterglow also work with CRT glass switched off.

GAUGES

The light controls now sit in their own groups. Use the bottom bar’s GAUGES for inherited settings, or a pane’s GAUGES to pin its own values.

The trail

Each pane retains only its replaced glyphs. A scroll, resize, theme or focus change, screen-buffer switch, hidden tab or covered terminal clears those old positions.
