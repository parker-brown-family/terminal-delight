2Decisions

Which glass?

Frost the panel (B), so the panes stay sharp while you pick, or frost everything around the tray (A), which TD already does for the reader.

B. Watching the pane change is the point of the tray. It costs one change to the frost shader in TD's renderer patch, and is built last, so the first two parts land without it.

Anything outside the 59: a theme published tomorrow, or one you build.

3The build

Difficulty goes from 4 to 5 because the glass touches the renderer. It is one pull request in three parts, each one working on its own:

  1. Omarchy is the list. Hide list, live re-read, the alacritty fallback, the 22 built-in copies with their drift test, and the colour sets out of the paint overlay and the favourites.
  2. The tray is the picker. The glyph map, the letter fallback, and the tray column rebuilt from the live list.
  3. The glass. The flipped frost and a see-through tray panel. If the shader change misbehaves, this part waits and the first two still ship.

It is checked by unit tests for each rule above, the CI gates (clippy -D warnings and rustfmt), and photographs of the real tray from a hidden test window: 18 tiles, then 17 after hiding one more theme.

Say go, or leave notes on the tiles and decisions and save them into the file. Part one starts in its own worktree off main as soon as one of those arrives, and the tray you open after it lands is your desktop's list.

How the thirty were ranked

GitHub, 2026-10-16

“Published” was read two ways at once. omarchy.org/themes lists 146 community themes and shows no popularity, and some of the most-starred themes on GitHub are not on it (DHH's Giants is one). So the candidates are both lists together: the 146 on the site, plus every repository named like an Omarchy theme in a GitHub search, 334 in all.

Each was ranked by its GitHub star count, read in one query on 2026-10-16. Tools that are not themes (Aether, theme hooks, launchers) and the upstream repositories of the 22 stock themes were taken out. The thirtieth place is Batou at 43; Futurism, Flexoki Dark and Event Horizon tie at 42 just below it.

Stars measure attention on GitHub, not installs. Nothing public counts installs, so this is the closest measurable stand-in inferred.
