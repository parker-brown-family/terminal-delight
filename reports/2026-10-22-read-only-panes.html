Read-Only Panes

This morning you hid a pane from agents, an agent asked for it back, and your Yes brought it back. That switch has two positions. 0.5.0 lets one agent hand work to another, and with two positions the only way to stop a managing agent assigning work to a pane is to hide the pane from it.

8 decisions waiting difficulty 6 / 10 · one plan, one approval nothing built · read from dev/0.5.0
Recommendation

Give each pane a middle position. Agents can watch a read-only pane, and cannot write to it or hand it work. A pane can only go lower than the window allows, and a position the window cannot read counts as off-limits, in this build and in older ones.
