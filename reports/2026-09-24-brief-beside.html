Brief Beside

You read an agent’s brief beside it, note it, and save. Then the agent re-reads the whole file to find what you wrote. This plan hands it your notes alone, and puts the brief beside the agent without a click.

Recommendation: paste only; the send stays yours.

Recommendation: yes, in the agent’s own tab only, floating instead at four panes.
