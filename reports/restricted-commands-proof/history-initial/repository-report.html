Restricted commands

A build script in an ordinary Terminal Delight pane has your account’s access, including routes to other panes. This change adds an explicit Restricted launch for offline shell/build work: the chosen project, read-only system tools and temporary home/tmp. It cannot open host controllers or new connections. Standard terminals keep their existing behavior. Online agents, TUIs and automatic handoffs are unavailable in this first profile.
