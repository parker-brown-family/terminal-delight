Yours to decide

What separator joins two appends?

A space is the cheap answer and is wrong for a paste that ends mid-word. A newline is right for two dictated sentences and wrong for a word completion.

Recommendation: a newline when the incoming chunk starts with a capital or the existing buffer ends in terminal punctuation; a space otherwise; nothing when the cursor is mid-word.

Is [Image #7] the protocol, or a leak?

It reads as an internal reference that escaped. It may equally be exactly how the attachment is meant to travel, in which case it needs to look like an attachment.

Recommendation: keep the token, render it as a chip in both the composer and the echo, and leave the wire format alone.

What stops the card growing?

Four lines fit. The fourth state in figure 05 is undecided, and undecided means it will be settled by whatever ships.

Recommendation: cap at eight lines of the pane’s current height, then scroll inside the card with the cursor pinned into view.
