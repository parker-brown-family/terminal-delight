01The picture

Choosing Rio was the easy half. Terminal Delight ran on alacritty's machinery, its read loop, its way of starting shells and its lock, as well as its core, and rio-vt cannot run inside any of it. So the swap gave Terminal Delight its own machinery first and changed the core second, and every step kept the old suite green.

Method and limits

what was and was not exercised

  • kitten icat itself was not run: kitty is not installed on this machine. A probe sent icat's own questions (the Kitty query with a device-attributes sentinel) and drew a PNG the way icat does. Issue 750 stays open for a real icat run.
  • htop is not installed; nvim stood in for a full-screen program.
  • No pointer or keyboard reached the windows. Tabs were switched by opening a new one through ctl adopt, which is how the forget-on-hide log line was produced. Coming back to the tab was not driven.
  • The old-host pairing used a host started from your build in its own session, never your live host.
  • The echo bench ran with nothing else building, two rounds per build alternating, one round with nineteen flooding panes. A saturating flood measures the scheduler as much as the terminal.
  • Locally, one test fails for a reason outside this change: the_vendored_fixtures_match_the_skill compares TD's copy of the brief fixtures with the installed decision-brief skill, which another session edited at 11:26 today.
