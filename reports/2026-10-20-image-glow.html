Image Glow

You liked the light spilling off the letters in cool-retro-term. That effect now has its own TD branch and a native slider. A separate test window is waiting on workspace 4, with a glowing pane beside a reference pinned at zero.

GAUGES → CRT → image glow

The dial

Open the bottom bar’s GAUGES button, expand CRT, and turn image glow. The outer control affects panes that follow it. Each pane can own a different strength through its existing GAUGES control; the right-hand reference already owns zero.

The light

The renderer reads bright neighbours from its existing scene texture and blends their light into each terminal’s image. Every sample is checked against that pane’s bounds. The effect uses sixteen neighbour samples per affected pixel and adds no history texture.
