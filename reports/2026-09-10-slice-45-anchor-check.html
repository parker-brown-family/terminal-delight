No field anywhere records that a connection greeted; a grep for greeted/negotiated/handshake state returns nothing. Every verb dispatches independently.

Who this bites

The socket is 0700 + SO_PEERCRED uid-checked, and a same-uid process can do worse than send verbs — so the weight of this finding sits on the version bump: the approved break path is “graceful shutdown, then TOML recovery — degrades to exactly today, once, deliberately” (Gate 2). That path runs through negotiation. While hello is optional, a stale client drives a new host, or the reverse, and the deliberate single degrade becomes an accident.

Invalidation

If a fresh control connection's first close-pane is refused for missing negotiation and the pane survives, this finding is stale. Today the pane dies — our own test asserts it.
