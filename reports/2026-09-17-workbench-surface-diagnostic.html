What the surface does with a page

Which way to build the row

recommended

A · Reuse the decision row, new kind

The artifact becomes one more row in the same list, with its own badge and an open verb.

Ships inside the rail that is already on screen: no new pane, no new focus rules. It costs you a rail that mixes two kinds of thing, and if artifacts get frequent that mixing is the first thing you will want undone.

B · A separate artifacts tab

The tab is already drawn in the header; give it the list of pages this session produced.

Genuinely better once a session makes many artifacts, and it keeps the decisions rail pure. It costs a tab nobody is looking at at the moment the page lands.

Yours to decide

1 · Rail row, or its own tab?

Both are cheap now and awkward to swap later, because the row kind leaks into how an agent reports a deliverable at all.

Recommendation: the rail row. The page lands while you are looking at the rail, and a tab you have to remember to open is a tab that stays shut.

2 · Do answered rounds collapse to one line?

The rail is the only place the space for an artifact row can come from, and a nine-round log is already taller than the pane.

Recommendation: collapse on answer, keep the answer text, expand on click.
